🍜 Yummy Maggi

A cozy bowl of happiness ✨

🛒 Ingredients

  • 🍜 1 packet Maggi
  • 💧 1½ cups water
  • 🫒 1 tablespoon oil
  • 🧅 1 chopped onion
  • 🍅 1 chopped tomato
  • 🫑 Some capsicum
  • 🌶️ Maggi tastemaker
  • 🧂 Salt

👨‍🍳 Cooking Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add onion and cook until soft.
  3. Add tomato and capsicum.
  4. Add water and bring it to a boil.
  5. Add Maggi noodles and tastemaker.
  6. Cook for 2–3 minutes.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy! 😋
